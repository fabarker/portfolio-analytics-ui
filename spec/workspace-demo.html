The workspace

Confirmed interaction direction · second iteration

The builder rail on the left is headed by the allocation being edited and split into four tabs: Build holds the allocation as a tree (category → product: each category under a silver rule with its colour tile, its products as leaves joined to it by elbow guides, a + on each category for quick add, and the unimplemented part of each category modelled at index level), Add searches the universe, Sleeves holds the repository, and Check lists what needs attention. The area on the right is a collection of cards from a card repository, led by a Headline card that sets forward-looking and historical figures side by side, each card stating its own basis, and sharing one context bar that states the context once, starting with the workspace’s reporting currency. The working mockup below is the reference for every flow in this document.

Try this: switch the rail’s header to Growth Tilt and open Check: Hedge Funds is modelled entirely at index level; press Find products to browse that category in Add, or open Sleeves and apply Core Alternatives, and the item leaves Check. Back on Strategic Core, in Build expand Public Equity and change a product weight; the category’s index remainder absorbs the difference and every card updates. Open a category’s menu (⋮) and choose Create sleeve from category, then on another allocation choose Apply a sleeve and compare “fill the target” with “fill only the index remainder”. From the ⋮ beside the allocation’s name choose Select products for a sleeve, tick products across two categories and create a mixed sleeve; or tick a few and press Save & compare to see the sleeve’s own analytics as a new column. Press + on Public Equity to add a product without leaving the tree: the search is limited to the category and the weight is prefilled with what the category holds at index level. Press Add card and browse the forward-looking, historical and structure groups; drag a card by its header, resize it from the corner, or use the arrow keys. In Drivers & differences pick a historical metric, change the period, open Scenarios. Open vs to pick a preset or custom benchmark, or create one; the benchmark library is at #/benchmarks. Switch a contribution card’s settings to Waterfall. At the bottom, the Active risk & return card splits each allocation into passive and active: open Risk sources, then switch the Factor model in the context bar to Axioma and watch only the factor cards update. Use a card’s menu to pin it to a model or to compare the two models side by side; a pinned card shows a model chip. Open About these figures at the foot of a card for its method, or turn them all on from the header’s ⋮ menu. The sleeve repository is at #/sleeves.

Layout contract

Keep the resizable rail available while cards change. Category expansion in the table changes every comparison column together. The selected table row drives the cards that follow it; legend emphasis is shared by the cards and independent of table columns. Pin identifiers while horizontally scrolling wide factor tables. Cards never overlap; the grid compacts after a move.

What changes, what stays

The context bar changes the reporting currency, factor model, period, reference, horizon and scenario for every card that reads them; a card’s basis is its own. A card’s settings change that card only. Both preserve row expansion, selection, emphasis and editing context. Creating or applying a sleeve changes the active allocation only; the repository is untouched by allocation edits.

Acceptance checks the mockup passes (node workspace/verify-workspace.mjs)
  • Lowering a product moves the difference to the category’s index remainder; raising a target above products shows an over flag; an edit that would exceed 100% without leverage is rejected with the resulting total.
  • Create sleeve from a category rescales its products to 100 and tags the category; a selection across categories creates a mixed sleeve filed under the chosen category.
  • Save & compare adds the new sleeve as a read-only, fully invested column in every card; a repository sleeve can be compared from the Add menu or from the repository, and a newer version can be shown in its column.
  • Apply a sleeve replaces the products or fills only the index remainder, tags them with the version, and is one undo step.
  • Card settings change one card; follow-the-selected-row retitles following cards; data tables mirror charts; adding from the repository, keyboard move and resize, mouse drag with collision, remove and undo, pack.
  • Context bar: period and scenario propagate to the right cards and no others; each control shows only when a card reads it; there is no Basis switch and each card states its own basis.
  • Rail: products are indented past their category and joined by elbow guides; quick add searches the category, prefills the remainder, adds in one edit, and Esc removes the row.
  • Benchmarks: one picker with presets and custom benchmarks; the builder saves only at 100%; versions are kept and a newer one is offered; the library lists, duplicates and versions them; cash + 3% earns cash plus 3%.
  • Reporting currency: required at creation, stated once, followed by cash and the risk-free rate, changeable and undoable.
  • Factor models: ISG’s eleven factors and Axioma’s seventeen; every new card draws; waterfalls add up to their totals.
  • Clarity: no tag on a live allocation; one door to scenarios; card tools, sort glyphs and rail weight boxes quiet at rest; a silver rule above each category; method notes closed by default and opened by the workspace preference; identifiers on a rail 420px or wider.
  • A workspace reloads with its allocations, cards and preferences; a sleeve edit in the repository creates v2 and archive/restore works.
  • A failed calculation keeps the previous result labelled and Retry recovers; a failed save says Not saved and offers retry.
  • No console errors at 1600, 1024 and 390px widths.

Mockup defaults Sixteen card types, Headline first; two factor models (ISG default, Axioma) with mock loadings and coverage; 80% band and 10-year horizon; quarterly rebalanced backtests; unallocated weight earns nothing; the index basket of each category is declared in the mock universe. Production cash, financing, derivative exposure and index-basket governance remain explicit open decisions.
