Rail colour studies

Ten colour treatments for the tabbed rail we agreed on (Build, Add, Sleeves, Check), plus today’s navy for reference. Every rail below works: switch tabs, expand categories, and type a weight. Weights are typed only: no stepper buttons, and nothing recalculates until you press Enter. Esc discards a typed value; ↑ and ↓ move between fields. The strip of workspace beside each rail shows the recalculation when you apply.

What stays fixed. The seven category colours (Public Equity blue, IG Fixed Income green and so on) are unchanged in every study, because the cards and charts use them to mean the same thing. Only the rail’s own surfaces, text, accent and states change. The contrast figures are measured from each palette (WCAG ratios), so a weak pairing shows up as a flag, not an opinion. One finding holds across all of them: on every dark rail the Private Equity purple is the faint swatch, and on the light rails five of the seven category colours fall below 3:1. That is a property of the category palette, so each swatch gets a hairline ring here.

Chosen: 9 · Split. Applied to the workspace mockup on 2 November 2026, with a hairline ring on every category swatch.

My recommendation was for a real change of character: 1 · Graphite & Saffron. It is the most neutral ground for the category colours: every text pairing passes AA and six of the seven category colours clear 3:1. If you want a change that still feels like the house: 9 · Split keeps the navy header and gives the tree a white body that reads as part of the card area, but the lighter category colours go faint on white, so it depends on the swatch rings. 7 · Stone & Navy is the light option with the same caveat.
