Data context without analytical clutter

Use one coherent cross-asset language, but do not manufacture comparability where coverage or conventions differ.

Always-visible meaning

Show the analytical basis, observation period or forecast horizon, currency, unit, scenario and reference close to every card: the context line states them in words and each card’s subtitle restates the ones it uses. Keep full methodology and source metadata available on demand. A shared factor label must mean the same factor and unit across all allocations.

Factor labels belong to a factor model: an ISG factor and its Axioma counterpart are different factors and are never shown together under one label.

A value of 0 means an observed or calculated zero. A dash means unavailable and has a reason. A negative contribution remains negative; do not normalize it away for a cleaner visualization.

Cross-asset differences

Private assets may have stale or smoothed values; derivatives may have notional and market-value exposures; funds may have partial look-through; an index held in a category is a basket of constituents, not a single product. These differences should appear as coverage or basis metadata, not separate disconnected applications.

The platform can disclose “proxy applied,” “quarterly observations,” “partial look-through,” “no category index” or “not comparable” at the affected row. Whether a proxy is permissible is a product/data policy, not an implied UI choice.

Behavior contracts for engineering

These contracts describe what the user must observe. They do not prescribe a backend architecture or a financial model. The mockup’s data-access layer (workspace/js/api.js, documented in workspace/README.md) lists every request the interface makes and the shape of each response. A workspace carries its categories as an ordered list [{id, name, color}]; every holding, product or index, carries its categoryId and weight; a category’s weight is derived as the sum of its holdings and is never stored or sent separately, and no part of a category is left to an index.

Verification at release

  • Exercise a four-to-six allocation comparison with factors and scenarios across a dozen cards.
  • Verify known totals and units against the supplied analytics outputs, including category totals and index holdings.
  • Test rapid consecutive edits and out-of-order responses per card.
  • Test keyboard operation, focus retention, screen-reader headers, contrast and reduced motion, including keyboard card layout.
  • Confirm restore, autosave failure, snapshot immutability and sleeve version immutability.
  • Test empty, missing, stale, invalid and failed results separately.

Mockup coverage versus product scope

The mockup implements the key interactions locally against a small coherent model. It does not implement user accounts, collaboration, trade execution, production imports, data connectors, actual-history reconstruction, full factor risk models or enterprise persistence. Those are not secretly provided by the placeholder calculations.

The 50-item ledger includes proposed refinements such as reordering columns, bulk paste, longer version history and exports. Their behavior is specified where material; not every refinement is simulated in the mockup or the teaching examples.
