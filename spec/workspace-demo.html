The workspace

Confirmed interaction direction · second iteration

The builder rail on the left is headed by the allocation being edited and split into four tabs: Build holds the allocation as a tree (portfolio → category → product, with the unimplemented part of each category modelled at index level), Add searches the universe, Sleeves holds the repository, and Check lists what needs attention. The area on the right is a collection of cards from a card repository, sharing one context bar. The working mockup below is the reference for every flow in this document.

Try this: switch the rail’s header to Growth Tilt and open Check: Hedge Funds is modelled entirely at index level; press Find products to browse that category in Add, or open Sleeves and apply Core Alternatives, and the item leaves Check. Back on Strategic Core, in Build expand Public Equity and change a product weight; the category’s index remainder absorbs the difference and every card updates. Open a category’s menu (⋮) and choose Create sleeve from category, then on another allocation choose Apply a sleeve and compare “fill the target” with “fill only the index remainder”. Press Select, tick products across two categories and create a mixed sleeve; or tick a few and press Save & compare to see the sleeve’s own analytics as a new column. Press Add card and place Stress periods and Rolling return and volatility; drag a card by its header, resize it from the corner, or use the arrow keys. Switch Basis to Historical, change the period, open Scenarios. The sleeve repository is at #/sleeves.

Layout contract

Keep the resizable rail available while cards change. Category expansion in the table changes every comparison column together. The selected table row drives the cards that follow it; legend emphasis is shared by the cards and independent of table columns. Pin identifiers while horizontally scrolling wide factor tables. Cards never overlap; the grid compacts after a move.

What changes, what stays

The context bar changes the analytical basis, period, reference, horizon and scenario for every card at once. A card’s settings change that card only. Both preserve row expansion, selection, emphasis and editing context. Creating or applying a sleeve changes the active allocation only; the repository is untouched by allocation edits.

Acceptance checks the mockup passes (node workspace/verify-workspace.mjs)
  • Lowering a product moves the difference to the category’s index remainder; raising a target above products shows an over flag; an edit that would exceed 100% without leverage is rejected with the resulting total.
  • Create sleeve from a category rescales its products to 100 and tags the category; a selection across categories creates a mixed sleeve filed under the chosen category.
  • Save & compare adds the new sleeve as a read-only, fully invested column in every card; a repository sleeve can be compared from the Add menu or from the repository, and a newer version can be shown in its column.
  • Apply a sleeve replaces the products or fills only the index remainder, tags them with the version, and is one undo step.
  • Card settings change one card; follow-the-selected-row retitles following cards; data tables mirror charts; adding from the repository, keyboard move and resize, mouse drag with collision, remove and undo, pack.
  • Context bar: basis, period and scenario propagate to the right cards and no others.
  • A workspace reloads with its allocations, cards and preferences; a sleeve edit in the repository creates v2 and archive/restore works.
  • A failed calculation keeps the previous result labelled and Retry recovers; a failed save says Not saved and offers retry.
  • No console errors at 1600, 1024 and 390px widths.

Mockup defaults Fourteen card types; 80% band and 10-year horizon; quarterly rebalanced backtests; unallocated weight earns nothing; the index basket of each category is declared in the mock universe. Production cash, financing, derivative exposure and index-basket governance remain explicit open decisions.
