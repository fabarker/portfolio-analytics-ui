Data context without analytical clutter

Use one coherent cross-asset language, but do not manufacture comparability where coverage or conventions differ.

Always-visible meaning

Show the analytical basis, observation period or forecast horizon, currency, unit, scenario and reference close to the table. Keep full methodology and source metadata available on demand. A shared factor label must mean the same factor and unit across all allocations.

A value of 0 means an observed or calculated zero. A dash means unavailable and has a reason. A negative contribution remains negative; do not normalize it away for a cleaner visualization.

Cross-asset differences

Private assets may have stale or smoothed values; derivatives may have notional and market-value exposures; funds may have partial look-through. These differences should appear as coverage or basis metadata, not separate disconnected applications.

The platform can disclose “proxy applied,” “quarterly observations,” “partial look-through” or “not comparable” at the affected row. Whether a proxy is permissible is a product/data policy, not an implied UI choice.

Production semantics to settle Currency conversion and hedging, stale-value thresholds, acceptable proxy coverage, derivative weighting, financing returns and negative-risk interpretation require explicit agreements. The frontend must be able to represent the resulting state without changing the core workflow.

Behavior contracts for engineering

These contracts describe what the user must observe. They do not prescribe a backend architecture or a financial model.

Verification at release

  • Exercise a four-to-six allocation comparison with factors and scenarios.
  • Verify known totals and units against the supplied analytics outputs.
  • Test rapid consecutive edits and out-of-order responses.
  • Test keyboard operation, focus retention, screen-reader headers, contrast and reduced motion.
  • Confirm restore, autosave failure and snapshot immutability.
  • Test empty, missing, stale, invalid and failed results separately.

Prototype coverage versus product scope

The live examples implement the key interactions locally. They do not implement user accounts, collaboration, trade execution, production imports, data connectors, actual-history reconstruction, full factor risk models or enterprise persistence. Those are not secretly provided by the placeholder calculations.

The 50-item ledger includes proposed refinements such as reordering columns, bulk paste, longer version history and exports. Their behavior is specified where material; not every refinement is simulated in the teaching examples.
